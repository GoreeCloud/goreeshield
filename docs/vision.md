## Purpose

GoreeShield is the privacy governance and user-choice layer across GoreeCloud. It should make personal-data behavior intelligible, controllable, and evidenced without collecting every user's private information into one central repository.

## Desired outcomes

1. Explain which product processes which data categories, for what declared purposes, with what recipients and retention.
2. Keep optional privacy-invasive collection, tracking, external transmission, and AI processing **off by default** until an informed, affirmative, scoped opt-in.
3. Enable withdrawal that stops future optional processing, and honestly describe what happens to previously processed data.
4. Orchestrate legitimate access, correction, portability, and deletion requests with per-product results and exceptions.
5. Provide receipts for important choices and data-rights outcomes, using minimal identifiers and bounded retention.
6. Help a person or household make privacy decisions without covert family-member monitoring.

## Boundaries

GoreeShield is **not** the identity provider (GoreeCloud Identity), secrets/password manager (GoreeCloud Vault), or threat/malware/security-monitoring platform (Wardveil). Its visual design is governed by GoreeCloud UI.

The privacy control plane must not claim universal OS permission overrides, universal tracker prevention, or blanket regulatory compliance. It must not replace the source-of-truth personal records in participating GoreeCloud applications.

## Working principles

Self-hosting and digital ownership; privacy by default; consent only where appropriate; least privilege; data minimization and purpose limitation; accessible native apps; accurate status, including incomplete work; maintenance sustainable by a sole maintainer.

## Classification

This is a proposed design direction. Product support, integrations, specific names, legal responsibilities, performance, and native release maturity remain to be established through implementation and verification.
